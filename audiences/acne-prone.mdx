## For acne sufferers tired of treatments that dry and damage their skin

Conventional acne treatment follows a "scorched earth" philosophy: benzoyl peroxide oxidizes everything, salicylic acid strips everything, Accutane shuts down oil production systemically. These approaches kill bacteria — but also destroy the skin barrier, trigger rebound oil production, and create a cycle of dryness-then-breakout that never resolves. Era Organics offers a gentler routine that cleanses, soothes, and moisturizes acne-prone skin while helping protect the skin barrier.

## The problem with mainstream acne treatment

### The barrier destruction cycle

Benzoyl peroxide generates free radicals that kill C. acnes — and also oxidize skin lipids, proteins, and DNA. TEWL increases 30-50% with regular BP use. The damaged barrier triggers compensatory sebum production. More sebum → more clogged pores → more acne → more BP applied → more barrier damage. The cycle perpetuates itself.

### Products that create dependency

Proactiv, Differin, and prescription retinoids require indefinite use — stopping produces "rebound" breakouts that are actually the skin returning to its damaged baseline. The treatment never allowed the barrier to repair, so removing the treatment reveals the underlying dysfunction that persists. A gentler approach puts barrier resilience first.

### Drying and irritation as "normal"

Tretinoin produces 4-12 weeks of peeling. Accutane requires blood monitoring for liver damage. The acne treatment industry has normalized significant side effects as acceptable trade-offs. These trade-offs exist because the treatments are indiscriminate — damaging everything rather than targeting C. acnes specifically.

## FAQ

### Does HOCl spray replace benzoyl peroxide?

Hypochlorous acid and benzoyl peroxide both act on C. acnes in lab studies — through different mechanisms. Benzoyl peroxide generates free radicals that damage bacterial and human cells indiscriminately. Hypochlorous acid has been studied for disrupting microbial cell membranes. Era Organics Hypochlorous Acid Spray is a cosmetic mist that cleanses and soothes skin, not a drug replacement for benzoyl peroxide — its formula is simply water, sodium chloride, and 0.02% hypochlorous acid, with no benzoyl peroxide.

### Will I purge on this routine?

Purging occurs with comedolytic agents (retinoids, BHA) that accelerate microcomedone surfacing. Some people notice changes in their skin during the first weeks of any new exfoliant, including the BHA AHA Serum. Purging is not new acne — it represents pre-existing clogs reaching the surface faster, and it settles as the backlog clears. If irritation persists, stop use and see a dermatologist.

### Is tea tree oil comedogenic?

Tea tree oil has a comedogenic rating of 1 (very low — unlikely to clog pores). The carrier formulation matters more than the tea tree oil itself. Era Organics Tea Tree Cream pairs tea tree oil with aloe and sodium hyaluronate in a moisturizing base. Studies of tea tree oil for acne-prone skin have typically used a 5% concentration.

### How long until acne clears?

Skin renews over several weeks, so give any routine 6-8 weeks of consistent use before judging how your skin looks. Comedonal acne (blackheads, closed comedones) involves microcomedones that form well before they show. Post-inflammatory marks typically fade over 3-6 months, and daily sunscreen helps. Persistent or inflamed acne is best evaluated by a dermatologist. Consistency matters more than intensity.

### Can I use this routine with prescription acne medication?

Check with your dermatologist before combining any skincare with prescription treatments (tretinoin, clindamycin, dapsone, spironolactone). If they approve, HOCl Spray can go on before prescription topicals. Tea Tree Cream moisturizes, which retinoid-treated skin often needs. BHA AHA Serum should be used on alternate days from prescription retinoids to avoid over-exfoliation. Face Wash Sensitive works as a gentle cleanser alongside an acne regimen.

### Does this work for hormonal acne?

Hormonal acne (deep cystic lesions on jawline and chin, cyclical with menstrual period) involves androgen-driven sebum overproduction that topical products cannot fully override. Era Organics products keep breakout-prone skin cleansed, moisturized, and comfortable while you address the cause. Severe hormonal acne additionally benefits from systemic approaches (spironolactone, oral contraceptives) that address the androgen driver directly.

### What about body acne (back, chest)?

Back and chest skin can follow the same four-step routine. HOCl Spray covers large areas efficiently (spray application vs. cream). Tea Tree Cream moisturizes and comforts skin in breakout-prone areas. BHA AHA Serum exfoliates to smooth the feel of skin on the back and chest. The lightweight spray format makes HOCl particularly practical for hard-to-reach areas like the mid-back.
