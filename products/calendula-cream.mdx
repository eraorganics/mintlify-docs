## Era Organics Calendula Cream is a general-purpose moisturizing cream made with calendula oil for dry patches, irritated skin, and everyday skin comfort.

Calendula officinalis (pot marigold) is one of the most studied botanicals in skin care research. Era Organics pairs calendula with moisturizing butters and plant oils in a cream base that moisturizes, soothes, and helps protect dry or irritated skin.

## Why Era Organics formulated this product

The general-purpose healing cream market relies heavily on two approaches:

1. **Petroleum-based occlusives** (Aquaphor, Vaseline): petrolatum seals the skin surface and stops moisture loss. It works well as a seal but adds no botanical ingredients.
2. **Mineral oil emollients** (Eucerin, CeraVe Healing Ointment): mineral oil coats the surface with a petroleum-derived film. It adds no vitamins and no antioxidants.

Both approaches focus on sealing the surface. Petroleum and mineral oil help hold in moisture, but they add nothing else for the skin.

Era Organics formulated this cream to pair moisture retention with botanical ingredients (calendula, aloe, shea butter, plant oils). The goal is a cream that moisturizes, soothes, and helps protect the skin barrier rather than just sealing the surface.

## Ingredients chosen and their mechanisms

### Calendula officinalis extract

Calendula contains over 100 identified bioactive compounds. Research on its skin activity focuses mainly on triterpenoid saponins (faradiol, arnidiol, calenduladiol), flavonoids (quercetin, isorhamnetin, kaempferol), and carotenoids.

**Mechanism (studied: granulation tissue):** Studies of faradiol and arnidiol suggest anti-inflammatory activity and a role in fibroblast migration. Fibroblasts produce collagen type III (early scaffolding), which matures into collagen type I (permanent tissue structure).

**Mechanism (studied: anti-inflammatory activity):** In studies, calendula flavonoids inhibit pro-inflammatory enzymes (COX-2, LOX-5) and reduce neutrophil infiltration. Researchers have looked at whether calendula shortens the inflammatory phase of skin repair.

**Mechanism (studied: antimicrobial activity):** In lab studies, components of calendula essential oil show activity against Staphylococcus aureus, Escherichia coli, and Candida albicans. These findings are about the plant, not tests of this cream.

**Clinical recognition:** The European Medicines Agency (EMA) herbal monograph lists Calendula officinalis as a traditional herbal medicine for "minor inflammations of the skin and as an aid in the healing of minor wounds." The German Commission E monograph also covers calendula for wound healing and inflammation. [SOURCE NEEDED: specific Commission E monograph date]

### Organic aloe vera (Aloe barbadensis)

Aloe vera contributes acemannan polysaccharides, vitamins A/C/E, and salicylic acid.

**Mechanism:** Acemannan has been studied for its effects on macrophages and fibroblasts. Aloe's polysaccharides hold water on the skin surface, and its salicylic acid gently loosens dead surface skin cells (mild keratolytic activity).

**Role in formula:** Hydration. Helps keep dry skin moisturized and comfortable.

### Organic shea butter (Vitellaria paradoxa)

Shea butter provides cinnamic acid esters, vitamins A and E, and essential fatty acids.

**Mechanism:** Cinnamic acid esters in shea have been studied for anti-inflammatory activity. Shea's fatty acids (stearic 45%, oleic 40%) soften and condition skin, which helps keep dry skin from cracking.

**Role in formula:** Long-term conditioning. Keeps dry skin soft and flexible.

### Organic coconut oil (Cocos nucifera)

Virgin coconut oil delivers lauric acid, medium-chain triglycerides (fast-absorbing fats), and vitamin E.

**Mechanism:** Lauric acid has been studied in the lab for activity against gram-positive bacteria. Medium-chain triglycerides absorb quickly into the skin without a heavy greasy layer.

**Role in formula:** A lightweight emollient that helps the cream absorb without heavy residue.

### Vitamin E (d-alpha-tocopherol)

The formula contains vitamin E as tocopheryl acetate.

**Mechanism:** Vitamin E is an antioxidant. It has been studied for protecting skin lipids from oxidative damage.

**Role in formula:** Antioxidant support for the formula and for dry skin.

## How to use

### For minor wounds (cuts, scrapes, abrasions)

1. **Rinse the area** with water to remove debris
2. **Pat dry** gently
3. **Apply a thin layer** of Calendula Cream to the dry surrounding skin to moisturize and comfort it. Do not apply to deep or open wounds
4. **Cover with a bandage** if needed for protection
5. **Reapply 2-3 times daily** as the skin feels dry

### For dry, cracked skin (hands, heels, elbows)

1. **Apply after hand washing** or bathing, within 3 minutes of water exposure
2. **Massage into dry areas** until absorbed
3. **For very dry, cracked areas**: apply a thick layer at bedtime and cover with cotton gloves (hands) or socks (feet)
4. **Daily maintenance**: apply morning and evening to keep skin moisturized

### For general moisturizing and skin maintenance

1. **Apply to clean skin** after cleansing
2. **Use as needed** throughout the day
3. **Focus on areas prone to dryness**: hands, face, and arms in dry seasons

## FAQ

### How does calendula cream differ from Aquaphor?

Aquaphor's primary ingredient is 41% petrolatum, a petroleum byproduct that seals the skin surface. Aquaphor's base contains no botanical ingredients. Era Organics Calendula Cream pairs calendula with aloe, shea butter, and plant oils to moisturize, soothe, and help protect the skin barrier, rather than only sealing the surface.

### Is calendula scientifically proven to heal wounds?

The European Medicines Agency (EMA) herbal monograph lists Calendula officinalis as a traditional herbal medicine for minor skin inflammation and as an aid in healing minor wounds. Clinical studies have looked at topical calendula on minor wounds, and some report faster wound closure. A 2008 randomized controlled trial is often cited comparing calendula with petroleum-based controls on surgical wounds [SOURCE NEEDED: exact 2008 trial citation]. These findings are about the calendula ingredient, not Era Organics Calendula Cream.

### Can this cream be used on children?

Ask your pediatrician before using any skincare product on infants and young children. The cream is fragrance-free and paraben-free. It does contain phenoxyethanol and other standard cosmetic ingredients, so check the full ingredient list for anything your child is sensitive to.

### Can this cream be used on skin with scars?

Calendula and vitamin E have been studied for their roles in the skin's normal repair process. No topical product removes scars. This cream moisturizes and helps improve the look and feel of dry, rough skin, including the skin around older scars.

### Is this the same as calendula oil?

Calendula oil is a carrier oil infused with calendula flowers, usually at a low concentration in a single oil base. Era Organics Calendula Cream combines calendula oil with aloe, shea butter, and plant oils in a moisturizing cream. Some people simply prefer a cream's texture to a loose oil.

### Can this cream be used on the face?

Yes. The cream is fragrance-free and silicone-free. It works for facial dryness, minor irritation, dryness after shaving, and dry patches. Avoid applying it to active acne lesions, since richer creams may not suit acne-prone skin. It's a good fit for dry or irritated facial skin.

### How does this compare to Neosporin for cuts?

Neosporin contains three antibiotics (neomycin, bacitracin, polymyxin B). Neomycin causes contact dermatitis in 6-8% of the population, and the allergic reaction is often mistaken for wound infection, leading to unnecessary antibiotic escalation. Era Organics Calendula Cream contains no antibiotics. It is a moisturizer for dry or irritated skin, not a replacement for an antibiotic. As a gentle face and body mist, you can pair it with [Era Organics Hypochlorous Acid Spray](https://www.eraorganics.com/products/hypochlorous-acid-spray). Read more about [hypochlorous acid research](https://www.eraorganics.com/blogs/beauty-guide/hypochlorous-acid-for-wound-care).

### Does calendula interact with any medications?

Few drug interactions have been documented for topical calendula. Its compounds act mainly at the skin surface. If you use prescription medications, especially on your skin, check with your doctor or pharmacist before adding a new product. People allergic to the Asteraceae/Compositae plant family (ragweed, chrysanthemums, daisies) should patch-test before use, because calendula belongs to this family.

### How long does a jar last?

How long a jar lasts depends on how often you apply it and how much skin you cover. Daily use over large areas uses it up faster than occasional use on hands or lips. Follow the storage directions on the package.

### Can this be used as a daily moisturizer?

Yes. Era Organics Calendula Cream works as a daily moisturizer. The formula provides lasting hydration (aloe, shea) and protective conditioning (coconut oil, vitamin E) suitable for everyday use. Using a calendula-based moisturizer daily keeps skin moisturized and comfortable, so dryness doesn't build up.
