## Era Organics Stretch Mark Oil is a plant-derived blend of rosehip seed, sunflower, borage, and olive oils with shea butter and vitamin E, formulated to moisturize and comfort skin during periods of rapid stretching.

Stretch marks (striae distensae) form when rapid skin expansion tears the dermis faster than collagen synthesis replaces structural fibers. Era Organics formulated this oil to moisturize, soften, and comfort skin during periods of rapid growth — pregnancy, weight fluctuation, puberty, and muscle building.

## Why Era Organics formulated this product

The stretch mark product market splits into two categories:

1. **Petroleum and mineral oil-based products** (Bio-Oil, Palmer's Cocoa Butter Formula) — coat the skin surface with occlusive petroleum derivatives. Mineral oil molecules are too large to penetrate the epidermis and reach the dermis where stretch marks form.
2. **Synthetic retinoid creams** (tretinoin, Retin-A) — effective but contraindicated in pregnancy (teratogenic), the exact period when many people look for stretch mark care.

Bio-Oil (Perrigo/Union-Swiss) contains mineral oil as its primary ingredient, synthetic fragrance, and artificial colorant (CI 26100 — a red dye). Palmer's Cocoa Butter Formula for Stretch Marks contains petrolatum, mineral oil, parabens (methylparaben, propylparaben), and synthetic fragrance.

Era Organics developed this oil for women (and men) who want a plant-based moisturizer during rapid skin expansion — including pregnant women looking for an option without synthetic retinoids.

## Ingredients chosen and their mechanisms

### Rosehip seed oil (Rosa canina)

Rosehip seed oil contains trans-retinoic acid (natural vitamin A), linoleic acid (44%), and alpha-linolenic acid (33%). A study in the International Journal of Cosmetic Science found rosehip oil significantly improved skin elasticity, moisture, and appearance of stretch marks over 8 weeks of use [SOURCE NEEDED: exact citation].

**Mechanism:** In rosehip oil, trans-retinoic acid has been studied for its effect on fibroblast activity and collagen type I and III production, at a lower concentration than prescription retinoids. Linoleic acid is a precursor to ceramide synthesis, part of the intercellular lipid matrix that holds skin structure together.

**Role in formula:** A moisturizing plant oil in the blend, chosen for the ingredient-level research on rosehip and skin elasticity.

### Vitamin E (d-alpha-tocopherol)

Natural vitamin E is a fat-soluble antioxidant that has been studied for protecting collagen fibers from oxidative degradation. Mechanical stress on dermal tissue generates reactive oxygen species (ROS) that can damage existing collagen.

**Mechanism:** Vitamin E integrates into cell membranes and protects polyunsaturated fatty acids from lipid peroxidation. Research has also looked at the compound's role in collagen cross-linking and blood flow in skin.

**Role in formula:** Listed in the formula as mixed tocopherols, an antioxidant ingredient that has been studied for skin.

### Jojoba oil (Simmondsia chinensis)

Jojoba oil is technically a liquid wax ester — structurally similar to human sebum. Jojoba provides gadoleic acid (C20:1), erucic acid (C22:1), and vitamin E. Jojoba is not listed in the current Era Organics Stretch Mark Oil ingredient list.

**Mechanism:** Jojoba is often discussed for its wax ester structure and its resemblance to the natural wax esters skin produces.

**Role in formula:** Not part of this formula; the blend uses sunflower oil, olive oil, and apricot seed oil to moisturize skin and keep it feeling soft.

### Argan oil (Argania spinosa)

Argan oil contains 80% unsaturated fatty acids (oleic acid 43%, linoleic acid 37%), squalene, tocopherols, and sterols (schottenol, spinasterol). A clinical study demonstrated argan oil improved skin elasticity by 14% after 60 days of topical application [SOURCE NEEDED: exact citation]. Argan is not listed in the current Era Organics Stretch Mark Oil ingredient list.

**Mechanism:** Squalene is a precursor to cholesterol synthesis in skin cells — cholesterol comprises 25% of the epidermal lipid barrier. Argan's sterols (schottenol, spinasterol) have been studied for their soothing properties. Linoleic acid supports ceramide production.

**Role in formula:** Not part of this formula; the blend includes shea butter, borage oil, and evening primrose oil to soothe skin and help protect the skin barrier.

## How to use

### For moisturizing stretching skin (pregnancy)

Many people begin daily application at the start of the second trimester (week 13), before visible skin expansion occurs. Apply to abdomen, breasts, hips, and thighs — all areas subject to expansion during pregnancy.

1. **After showering** — apply to damp skin
2. **Warm 4-5 drops** between palms
3. **Massage into target areas** using circular motions for 2-3 minutes per area
4. **Apply twice daily** — morning and evening for consistent moisture

### For areas with existing stretch marks

Newer stretch marks (red, pink, or purple) differ from older ones (white or silver), which are generally harder to change in appearance. Start moisturizing these areas whenever you like.

1. **Apply 3-4 drops** directly to the area
2. **Massage firmly** in perpendicular direction to the stretch marks for 3-5 minutes
3. **Apply twice daily** — three times daily if skin feels dry
4. **Maintain consistency** as part of your daily routine

### For growth spurts (adolescents)

Apply to hips, breasts, thighs, and buttocks during active growth phases. Apply once daily to keep skin moisturized, or twice daily during rapid growth periods.

## FAQ

### Does this oil actually prevent stretch marks?

No topical product guarantees stretch mark prevention. Genetics, rate of expansion, cortisol levels, and skin type all influence susceptibility. Some research has looked at topical vitamin E, rosehip oil, and essential fatty acids during pregnancy and stretch mark incidence and severity [SOURCE NEEDED: meta-analysis citation]. Era Organics Stretch Mark Oil is a cosmetic oil: it moisturizes and comforts stretching skin, and it does not prevent or treat stretch marks.

### Is this oil safe during pregnancy?

The formula is made from plant oils and butters, including rosehip seed oil, sunflower oil, shea butter, and mixed tocopherols (vitamin E), and is free of parabens, phthalates, synthetic fragrance, and synthetic dyes. It also contains sweet orange oil, sweet lavandin essential oil, and rosemary extract, so review the full ingredient list with your healthcare provider before using it during pregnancy.

### How is this different from just using coconut oil?

Coconut oil is primarily lauric acid (C12:0) — a saturated medium-chain fatty acid that sits on the skin surface. Era Organics' blend combines a wider range of plant oils — rosehip seed, borage, evening primrose, tamanu, sunflower, and olive — with shea butter and vitamin E. Coconut oil moisturizes on its own; this formula layers several oils and butters for moisture and comfort.

### Why oil instead of cream/lotion for stretch marks?

An oil puts plant oils directly on the skin with no water phase. Creams are oil-in-water emulsions where the water evaporates and oils make up only part of the formula. This oil is made from plant oils, butters, extracts, and vitamin E. Lotions also require emulsifiers, preservatives, and stabilizers that provide no therapeutic benefit.

### Can men use this product?

Yes. Stretch marks affect anyone experiencing rapid body size changes. Male bodybuilders frequently develop striae on shoulders, upper arms, and thighs. Adolescent males develop stretch marks on the back and thighs during growth spurts. The formula contains no gender-specific ingredients.

### Does this oil stain clothing?

Plant oils can transfer to fabric until they absorb. Era Organics Stretch Mark Oil contains no artificial colorants (unlike Bio-Oil, which contains red dye CI 26100 that transfers to fabric). Allow full absorption before dressing. Applying to damp post-shower skin helps the oil spread easily.

### When should I start using it during pregnancy?

Many people begin at the start of the second trimester (week 13), before visible expansion. Starting early keeps skin moisturized and supple as it stretches. You can also start after stretch marks appear to keep the area moisturized and comfortable.

### Can this oil be combined with other Era Organics products?

The oil goes on first; the cream layers on top to help seal in moisture. Pregnant users may also consider [Era Organics Organic Nipple Butter](https://www.eraorganics.com/products/organic-nipple-cream), a USDA Organic certified balm.

### How long until I notice a difference in how my skin feels?

Results vary from person to person, and this oil is a cosmetic moisturizer, not a treatment. Consistent twice-daily use keeps the skin around stretch marks moisturized and soft. Older stretch marks (white/silver) are generally harder to change in appearance than newer ones (red/purple).

Era Organics Stretch Mark Oil contains no synthetic fragrance. Its natural scent comes from the plant oils in the blend, including sweet orange oil, sweet lavandin essential oil, and rosehip seed oil.
