## The key ingredients

### Tea tree oil (Melaleuca alternifolia)

In Tea Tree Cream, tea tree oil helps reduce the appearance of redness and irritation and helps keep skin looking clean and clear.

### Salicylic acid

Salicylic acid is a beta-hydroxy acid (BHA). It helps exfoliate dead skin, clear out clogged pores and improve the look of skin texture.

### Willow extract

Willow extract is a natural BHA. It works alongside salicylic acid for gentle exfoliation and helps balance the look of oily skin.

### Aloe vera (Aloe barbadensis leaf juice)

Aloe leaf juice is the first ingredient in the formula. It naturally contains vitamins and minerals and gives deep hydration while calming the look of irritated skin.

### St. John's Wort

St. John's Wort is a flowering plant used in skincare to soothe skin and calm the look of irritation, a common choice for blemish-prone skin.

### Pomegranate seed oil

Pomegranate seed oil contains the omega-5 fatty acid. It helps improve the look of skin texture.

### Goldenseal, burdock root and dandelion root

These three botanicals round out the formula. Goldenseal helps cleanse skin and is high in berberine. Burdock root helps improve the look of skin quality and complexion. Dandelion root helps balance the look of oily, blemish-prone skin.
