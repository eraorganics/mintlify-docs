## Ingredients chosen and their mechanisms

### Tripeptide blend

Peptides are short amino acid chains that have been studied for signaling fibroblasts to produce collagen. Era Organics includes a tripeptide blend in the formula as part of its lip-conditioning base.

**Mechanism:** Matrixyl-type peptides (palmitoyl pentapeptide-4 and related sequences) have been studied for binding to TGF-beta receptors on dermal fibroblasts and triggering pro-collagen I and III synthesis. A study published in the International Journal of Cosmetic Science reported that palmitoyl pentapeptide-4 increased collagen synthesis by 117% in fibroblast cultures [SOURCE NEEDED: exact study citation and verify percentage].

**Lip-specific relevance:** Lip vermillion (the red/pink portion) has thinner dermis than facial skin — only 3-5 cell layers versus 16 layers on the cheek. Collagen loss in this thin tissue produces visible volume reduction faster than elsewhere on the face.

**Role in formula:** Conditioning active. Part of a daily lip care routine that keeps lips feeling soft and looking smoother with consistent use.

### Hyaluronic acid

Hyaluronic acid (HA) is a glycosaminoglycan that holds up to 1,000x its weight in water. Era Organics includes hyaluronic acid to moisturize the lips.

**High molecular weight HA (>1,000 kDa):** In skincare generally, forms a hydrating film on the skin surface, retaining moisture and giving a fuller look through surface hydration.

**Low molecular weight HA (&lt;50 kDa):** In skincare research, smaller HA molecules are studied for reaching deeper into the epidermis and attracting water into the tissue.

**Mechanism:** HA molecules attract water through hydrogen bonding. Each disaccharide unit binds 2-3 water molecules. In tissue, this water retention expands the extracellular matrix volume. Low-weight HA has also been studied for signaling fibroblasts to produce HA.

**Role in formula:** Hydration. Moisturizes the lips so they look smoother and feel softer.

### Plant-derived squalane

Squalane (hydrogenated squalene) is commonly derived from olive oil; this formula uses extra virgin olive oil itself for emollient conditioning.

**Mechanism:** Squalane mirrors the lipid composition of human sebum (12% of skin surface lipids are squalene). Lip tissue produces minimal sebum, which is why plant oils are a staple of lip care for maintaining the lip's lipid barrier.

**Role in formula:** Extra virgin olive oil provides emollient conditioning and helps protect the lip barrier. Conditions and softens the feel of lip texture. Learn more about [squalane](https://www.eraorganics.com/blogs/beauty-guide/hydrating-ingredient-squalane) as a skincare ingredient.

### Vitamin E (d-alpha-tocopherol)

Vitamin E is a common antioxidant in lip care; it is not a listed ingredient in this formula, which relies on sunflower oil and extra virgin olive oil for its emollient base.

**Mechanism:** Vitamin E has been studied for integrating into cell membranes and neutralizing free radicals that damage collagen fibers. Lip tissue receives 3-5x more UV exposure than surrounding facial skin (no melanin production in vermillion border).

**Role in formula:** Not part of this formula. Sunflower oil and extra virgin olive oil condition the lips and help protect the lip barrier.

### Coconut oil, castor oil and beeswax

Coconut oil, castor oil and beeswax provide the emollient base, conditioning, and help protect the barrier of dry lips.

**Mechanism:** Coconut oil's medium-chain triglycerides absorb readily into the thin lip epidermis. Beeswax and castor oil give the gloss its body on the lip surface.

**Role in formula:** Base conditioning and comfort. Moisturizes the dryness and flaking that make lips appear thinner. Smooth, hydrated lip surface reflects light differently than dehydrated lips, creating a fuller look.

### Why do irritant plumpers damage lips long-term?

Repeated inflammatory activation triggers matrix metalloproteinase (MMP) release — enzymes that break down collagen. Chronic capsaicin/cinnamon exposure to lip tissue creates a cycle: inflammation damages collagen, lips thin further, user increases product use, more collagen degrades. The product designed to create fullness systematically destroys the protein responsible for natural volume.

### Can men use this product?

Yes. Lips can look thinner and drier with age regardless of gender. The formula is fragrance-free and synthetic-dye-free. It moisturizes and conditions lips for anyone who uses it.

### How does this compare to collagen supplements (pills/powder)?

Oral collagen supplements provide amino acids systemically — distributed across the entire body. Topical lip care works right at the application site: Era Organics' formula pairs a tripeptide blend with hyaluronic acid and plant oils to moisturize and condition the lips directly. The two approaches work through different pathways (systemic amino acid supply vs. topical lip care).
