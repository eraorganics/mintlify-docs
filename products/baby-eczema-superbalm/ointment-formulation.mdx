## What is ointment formulation?

Ointment formulation is a pharmaceutical formulation: the way an oil-and-wax base is built and the active ingredients are blended into it. It goes by a few names in practice, an ointment recipe, a topical formulation, or simply the making of a topical ointment, but the core work is the same. The base decides how the product feels, how well it seals the skin, and how few extra ingredients it needs. Era Organics formulates its Baby Eczema Superbalm as an ointment so the base itself does protective work. Superbalm temporarily protects and helps relieve itch and irritation from eczema, and its base also carries gluten free oats, chamomile, and calendula oil.

The choice of base is the first decision. A water-heavy cream needs emulsifiers and preservatives to stay stable; an oil-and-wax ointment needs far fewer. Fewer added ingredients means fewer potential irritants.

## The parts of the ointment formulation process

The ointment formulation process comes down to a handful of decisions, and a few of them are things you can put a number or a target on. The first is active ingredient concentration, the amount of the working ingredient carried in the base; in Superbalm, that base also carries gluten free oats alongside chamomile and calendula oil. The second is viscosity, how thick the finished ointment is, which an oil-and-wax base pushes toward the rich, semi-solid end so it stays on the surface rather than running off. The third is the pH level, which formulators generally aim to keep in a mild, skin-friendly range so a product is gentle on an already stressed barrier. Set those three, active concentration, viscosity, and pH, and the rest of the recipe follows from them. It is a deliberate build, not a one-size mix.

## How Superbalm's base is built

Superbalm's base combines plant butters, oils, and wax. Cocoa butter and coconut oil give it body and emollient richness; [beeswax](/ingredients/beeswax) sets the structure and seals the surface. Gluten free oats, chamomile, and calendula oil are carried within that base.

Because the base is oil-and-wax rather than a water emulsion, it needs fewer added ingredients, and Superbalm is fragrance-free, paraben-free, phenoxyethanol-free, and mineral-oil-free. The emollients that make up the base are covered in [coconut oil](/ingredients/coconut-oil).

## A built recipe, not an off-the-shelf mix

An ointment formulation is the opposite of a ready-made preparation grabbed off a shelf and relabeled. An off-the-shelf product is a generic base that a brand simply pours a scent or color into; a purpose-built topical ointment is designed from the base up around the job it has to do. For eczema-prone skin that difference matters, because a formula assembled deliberately can leave out the emulsifiers, fragrances, and preservatives a stock base would carry by default. Superbalm's recipe is built on plant butters, oils, beeswax, and gluten free oats rather than a water-based cream.

## Frequently asked questions about ointment formulation

### Why does an ointment need fewer preservatives?

Water in a formula invites microbial growth, so water-based creams need preservatives. An oil-and-wax ointment holds little to no water, so it needs far fewer. Superbalm uses that oil-and-wax approach and is paraben-free and phenoxyethanol-free.

### What is the difference between an ointment and an off-the-shelf cream?

A purpose-built ointment is a topical formulation designed from its base up for a specific job, while an off-the-shelf product is a generic base with a name added. The built recipe can leave out ingredients a stock cream carries by default.

### What makes up the Superbalm base?

Plant butters and oils like cocoa butter and coconut oil provide the emollient body, and beeswax sets the structure and seals the surface. Gluten free oats, chamomile, and calendula oil are blended into that base. It is fragrance-free and mineral-oil-free.

### Does the formulation affect how it feels?

Yes. An oil-and-wax base feels richer than a lotion and stays on the surface longer. Superbalm's base is built from cocoa butter, coconut oil, and beeswax. That staying power is the goal for a protective balm.
