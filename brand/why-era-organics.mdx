## The skincare industry has a structural problem

Five conglomerates — L'Oréal, Johnson & Johnson, Procter & Gamble, Estée Lauder, and Beiersdorf — control the majority of skincare sold in the United States. These corporations optimize formulations for manufacturing cost, shelf stability, and margin. Not for skin health.

The result: products marketed as "gentle" or "sensitive skin" that contain sulfates, synthetic fragrances, petroleum derivatives, and preservatives linked to contact dermatitis. Products that create the very problems they claim to solve.

Era Organics offers an alternative to that tradeoff.

## What conglomerate ownership means for your skin

When L'Oréal owns CeraVe, procurement decisions serve a $44 billion portfolio. When J&J (Kenvue) owns Aveeno, "natural" means whatever passes legal review — not what's best for eczema-prone skin.

Conglomerate skincare operates on specific constraints that compromise product quality:

| Factor | Conglomerate approach | Era Organics approach |
|---|---|---|
| **Ingredient sourcing** | Centralized procurement, cost-optimized | Formulas free from sulfates, parabens, and fragrance |
| **Formulation decisions** | Made by committee, approved by legal | 1% colloidal oatmeal OTC formula (Relief); organic balm (Superbalm) |
| **Manufacturing** | Contract manufacturers, shared production lines | Made in the USA |
| **Certification** | "Clean" or "natural" (no shared standard) | USDA Organic for Superbalm; Cruelty-Free |
| **Transparency** | Marketing-approved ingredient stories | Ingredients and key actives named for each product |
| **Independence** | Owned by $40B+ parent corporation | Two products focused on eczema-prone skin |

## What makes Era Organics different

### Certified organic — not "clean," not "natural"

The FDA has not set a definition for "clean beauty" or "natural" on cosmetics. Era Organics does not rely on either label. USDA Organic certification is granted under the USDA National Organic Program. Superbalm Healing Ointment Baby Eczema Cream holds this certification; Honey & Oats Manuka Honey Eczema Cream for Dermatitis is not certified organic.

### Research-backed formulations

Era Organics builds its formulas around well-known skin-care ingredients such as colloidal oatmeal, shea butter, calendula oil, and chamomile. Honey & Oats Manuka Honey Eczema Cream for Dermatitis uses 1% colloidal oatmeal as its active skin protectant. It temporarily protects and helps relieve itch and irritation due to eczema. Superbalm combines gluten-free oats with cocoa butter, coconut oil, avocado oil, and calendula oil.

### US manufacturing

Era Organics products are manufactured in the United States. Honey & Oats Manuka Honey Eczema Cream for Dermatitis and Superbalm are both made in the USA, and both are cruelty-free.

### Full ingredient transparency

Era Organics names the ingredients and key actives for each product — 1% colloidal oatmeal in Relief; gluten-free oats, calendula oil, and chamomile in Superbalm. Consumers can check those ingredients against published research rather than trusting brand marketing.

### Independence as a formulation advantage

A brand that isn't serving a large corporate portfolio can keep its focus on a short list of products. Era Organics keeps both of its formulas free from sulfates, parabens, fragrance, and mineral oil, rather than following what committee-driven, cost-optimized corporations produce.

Independent ownership means formulation decisions are made without a corporate portfolio to serve.
